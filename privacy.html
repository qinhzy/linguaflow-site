生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-09-21 · 适用于 iOS、Android 与桌面版

中文

我们收集什么

LinguaFlow 不设账户、不含统计或广告 SDK，也不向开发者上传任何数据。你的翻译历史、收藏、术语库、草稿与设置全部保存在你自己设备上的应用沙盒内（可随时在「设置」中导出备份或清空历史）。

翻译文本去了哪里

当你使用在线翻译引擎时，待翻译的文本会由应用直接发送到你在「设置」中启用的服务（Google 翻译、MyMemory、LibreTranslate、DeepL、OpenAI 兼容大模型、百度翻译等），这些服务各自的隐私政策适用于该次传输。离线词典与常用语手册完全在本机运行，不联网。只需离线使用时，可在设置中关闭所有在线引擎。

API 密钥

你填写的引擎密钥仅保存在本机设置文件中，仅用于向对应服务发起请求；应用的备份导出默认不包含密钥。

系统权限

应用不请求相机、麦克风、通讯录或位置权限。语音朗读使用系统自带的语音合成；「分享到译流」/「翻译选中文字」仅接收你主动分享的文字。

儿童

本应用面向一般用户，不针对 13 岁以下儿童收集任何信息。

联系方式

如有疑问，请通过应用商店页面的开发者联系方式与我们联系。

English

What we collect

LinguaFlow has no accounts, no analytics or advertising SDKs, and never uploads anything to the developer. Your translation history, favourites, glossary, drafts and settings stay in the app's sandbox on your own device (you can export a backup or clear the history from Settings at any time).

Where translated text goes

When you use an online engine, the text you translate is sent directly from the app to the services you enabled in Settings (Google Translate, MyMemory, LibreTranslate, DeepL, an OpenAI-compatible LLM, Baidu Translate, …); each service's own privacy policy applies to that transfer. The offline dictionary and phrasebook run entirely on your device. Disable every online engine in Settings if you only want offline use.

API keys

Keys you enter are stored only in the app's local settings and are used solely to call the corresponding service; exported backups omit them by default.

Permissions

The app does not request the camera, microphone, contacts or location. Read-aloud uses the system speech synthesizer; "Share to LinguaFlow" / "Translate selection" only receive text you deliberately share.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Contact

Questions? Reach us through the developer contact on the app store listing.
